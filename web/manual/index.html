Velanzo — how to use it

For the owner, the shops and the warehouse. Every screen, with a picture.

1. How Velanzo works

Velanzo follows your stock from the supplier to the customer. The warehouse enters what arrives, puts a barcode label on every piece and sends parcels to the shops. The shops receive the parcels, scan items to make bills, and take returns. Everything is recorded on its own, so the owner sees every shop and the warehouse at any time — on a laptop or a phone.

#StepWhoWhere in Velanzo
1Stock comes from a supplierWarehousePurchase
2It is added to the warehouse stock—automatic
3A barcode label goes on every pieceWarehouseLabels
4A parcel is packed and sent to a shopWarehouseDispatch order
5The shop checks the parcel and receives itShopReceive stock
6–8Items are scanned, paid and a bill is printedShopSell

Every computer keeps its own copy

Each shop counter, the warehouse computer and the owner's phone keep their own copy of what they need, and the cloud keeps one shared copy of everything. When there is no internet, keep working: the bills and changes wait on that computer and go up to the cloud by themselves when the internet is back. No computer has to stay on at night.

Who can do what

The owner decides what each shop and the warehouse may do with on/off switches (Settings → Shops & warehouse). Each person can then have fewer switches than their place (Settings → People). A shop's people only see that shop. Only what is switched on appears on their screen — and the cloud itself refuses anything that is switched off.

SwitchA shop (usual)The warehouse (usual)
Sell and print bills · Returns and exchanges · Give discountsOnOff
Change prices on a bill · Cancel billsOffOff

Buy from suppliers · Send stock · Add new items and print labels · Correct stock countsOffOn

Example: to stop a shop buying from suppliers itself, keep Buy from suppliers off for that shop. To let one trusted shop manager cancel bills, switch Cancel bills on for the shop, then off for the other people there.

2. Starting (owner, once)

Open your Velanzo address in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (for example https://yourname.github.io/velanzo). The same address works on every computer and phone.

2.1 Your owner account

  1. Choose I'm the owner.
  2. The very first time: click New business? Create an owner account, type your email and a password (at least 8 characters). If an email arrives, open its link, then come back and sign in.
  3. Type your business name and your name, and choose a passcode on the number pad: exactly 4 numbers, typed twice. You type it every time Velanzo opens, like a phone.
Keep your email and password safe. They open Velanzo as the owner on any new phone or computer. Your passcode only works on computers that are already set up.

2.2 Shops and their switches

More → Settings → Shops & warehouse. Velanzo starts with Main warehouse and Shop 1. Rename them, add their address and phone (printed on their bills), and add more shops when you open them.

2.3 Users: ID, password and passcode

Everyone who works in Velanzo is a user, in one list: Settings → Users → Add a user. Type their name, where they work, and make up a user ID and password (for example ahmed.shop1). Tell them both. Leave Same as their place ticked, or untick it to switch some things off for this person only.

The list also shows, for every user, their computer, when it was last online and whether they have chosen their passcode. The passcode is the user's own secret: the owner never types it and never sees it.

Someone leaves? Open them and untick Can sign in. Their history stays. A new password? Open them and type one. A computer was lost or replaced? Click Remove computer on their row: it stops working at once.

2.4 A user signs in on their computer

  1. On the shop or warehouse computer open the Velanzo address in Chrome or Edge, choose I work at a shop or the warehouse and type the user ID and password.
  2. Velanzo downloads that place's items and stock.
  3. The user chooses their own 4-digit passcode on the number pad (typed twice) and is in.
  4. From then on this computer belongs to that user. It stays signed in, but asks the passcode every time Velanzo opens or refreshes. On that screen, Switch account or Sign out lets another person sign in (it first makes sure nothing is waiting to be sent).

The owner signs in the same way with their email and password on each owner computer, and also chooses a passcode there.

3. Opening Velanzo every day

Open Velanzo and type your 4-digit passcode on the number pad (it opens by itself at the 4th number). To step away: your name (top right) → Lock. When someone else takes over this computer: Switch account or Sign out, on the same menu or on the passcode screen.

The green pill at the top tells you if everything reached the cloud:

Top bar saysMeaning
● All saved onlineEverything done here is in the cloud.
● Saving… / 2 waitingBeing sent right now.
● No internet · 3 waitingKeep working. The 3 changes go up when the internet is back.
● 1 not savedThe cloud refused a change (for example a switch was off). Tap it to see why.
● Practice modeYou are in Training. Nothing is real and nothing is sent. See below.

Forgot your passcode or password?

This computer stays signed in; only the passcode is asked, each time Velanzo opens. If you forget it:

  • Shop or warehouse person: on the PIN screen tap Forgot your PIN? → Send the link. A link goes to the owner's email. The owner opens it (on any phone or computer) and presses Approve. Your screen moves on by itself: choose a new PIN. The owner never sees it.
  • Owner: the same on the owner's computer or phone; the link goes to the owner's own email. Forgot the account password: Owner sign in → Forgot your password?.
  • Forgot the password of a user? The owner types a new one in Settings → Users.

The owner's email must be a real, working address — it receives these links. Links work for 30 minutes.

Learning by doing: Training

New here? Open More → Training (or your name → Training). Pick a guide — Full overview, Making a bill, Purchase, Send stock, Receiving a parcel and more — and Velanzo shows an arrow where to click and says what to do. It moves on by itself when you have done each step, and you only see guides for what your place and your switches allow.

Guides marked practice use the real screens with DEMO items. An orange bar shows that practice is on; nothing is sent online and nothing prints. When you press Finish & remove practice everything you did is deleted completely, as if it never happened. Do not make real sales while practising — they would be deleted too.

4. Shop

4.1 Make a bill

  1. Scan each item (or type its name and press Enter). Scanning the same item again adds one more.
  2. Change a number with − and +, or remove a line with ✕.
  3. Choose Cash, Card or Online. For cash, type what the customer gave: the change shows in green.
  4. Press ✓ Done — print bill (or F9). The bill prints.

Discount and Customer (name and phone) are the small links under the list. They only show if your switches allow them.

4.2 Return or exchange

  1. Tap ↩ Return or exchange (or F4) and scan what the customer brings back. It shows as Back.
  2. Tap it again and scan the new item they take, if any.
  3. The total says what the customer pays, or what to give back (in red). Press Done.

4.3 Find a bill and print it again

Bills: today's bills, or choose dates. Scan the barcode on the customer's bill, or type its number, name or phone. Click a bill to see it and print it again.

4.4 Receive stock

When the warehouse sends a parcel, Receive stock shows a number. Open the parcel, then either scan every piece (Velanzo counts them) or correct the Received numbers. Press ✓ Confirm received. Missing pieces are shown to the owner.

4.5 Expenses

Expenses: tea, transport, bills, rent. They are taken off the profit in the owner's reports.

5. Warehouse

The warehouse home has one big button for each job, and shows the parcels on the way.

5.1 Purchase

  1. Purchase → New purchase. Choose the supplier and type their bill number.
  2. Scan items you already have, or click New item to add a new design with its sizes and colours (Velanzo makes a barcode for each).
  3. Type how many came and their cost. Type what you paid the supplier now (0 if later — it shows as owed).
  4. Press Save purchase & add to stock, then print the labels.

5.2 Items

More → Items: every item with its barcode and price. Click one to change its price or see where every piece went.

5.3 Barcode labels

Labels: choose a purchase (or scan items), set how many labels, and print. Stick one on every piece.

5.4 Send stock to a shop

  1. Send stock → Dispatch order. Choose the shop.
  2. Scan each piece as you pack it — every scan adds one.
  3. Press Send dispatch and print the dispatch note for the parcel.

6. Owner

6.1 Home — everything live

Today's sales, this month's profit and what you owe suppliers. Below, a card for every shop and the warehouse: who is working there now, today's sales or parcels, and the last thing that happened. It updates by itself every few seconds.

6.2 Activity

More → Activity: every bill, return, purchase, parcel, stock correction and expense, at every place, with who and when. Choose a day or a place.

6.3 Reports and profit

Reports: sales, returns, discounts, cost, profit, expenses and net profit, for any dates, for the whole business or one shop; plus best sellers, categories and day by day. Print it or take the bill lines to Excel.

Net sales = items sold − returns − discounts
Gross profit = net sales − what those items cost
Net profit = gross profit − expenses

6.4 Stock and corrections

Stock: every item at every place. After counting the shelves, use Correct to fix a number (damaged, lost, found) with a reason — it is kept in the item's history.

6.5 Users and their computers

Settings → Users lists every user with their user ID, place, computer, when it was last online and whether they chose a passcode. Remove computer on a lost or replaced computer: it stops working at once, and the user signs in again on the new one.

6.6 Backups and Excel

Settings → Backup & data: Download full backup saves one file with everything. Keep copies on Google Drive or a USB stick — Velanzo reminds you on the home screen when your last backup is more than a week old. Restore replaces everything (on every device) with a backup file, also one from the old version of Velanzo. You can also export items, stock, bills, purchases and more to Excel, and import many items at once.

Manage data: clear old data or start fresh

Settings → Backup & data → Manage data shows how much of everything is stored, and lets the owner clear it:

  • Clear some data — tick what to clear (bills, purchases, stock sent, expenses, stock history) and choose dates: a from and an up to date, or a quick pick like “older than 3 months”. Your items, stock counts, people and settings stay. Parcels still on the way are never cleared.
  • Clear all data — deletes all bills, purchases, stock, items and suppliers to start the real business fresh. Shops, warehouse, people, PINs and settings stay.

Before anything is deleted Velanzo shows exactly what will go, downloads a backup file of everything, and asks you to type CLEAR. The clearing is then done on every computer. To undo it, restore that backup file.

7. No internet?

  • Keep working. Selling, returns, receiving, stock arrived, sending parcels and expenses all work without internet.
  • The top bar shows No internet · N waiting. When the internet is back, everything goes up by itself — you don't have to do anything.
  • Other places don't see your changes until then (for example the owner's sales total, or a parcel you sent).
  • Don't clear the browser's data and don't remove Velanzo while something is waiting — it would be lost. Installing Velanzo (2.5) protects it.
  • The very first set-up of a computer needs the internet once.

8. Printers and scanners

Barcode scanner

Any USB scanner works — it types the barcode and presses Enter, like a keyboard. Plug it in and scan into the box on the Sell screen.

Receipt printer (shop)

Set the 80 mm (or 58 mm) receipt printer as the default printer in Windows. In the print window choose Margins: None and untick Headers and footers once.

Print without the print window: make a shortcut for Chrome and add --kiosk-printing at the end of its Target, after the Velanzo address. Bills then go straight to the default printer.

Label printer (warehouse)

In the printer's Windows settings, make a paper size the same as your labels (for example 50 × 25 mm), and type the same size in Settings → Bills & labels. A normal printer with A4 sticker sheets also works.

9. Daily checklists

Shop

  • Open Velanzo and type your passcode.
  • Check Receive stock for parcels.
  • Sell; keep the bills' copies if you need them.
  • Add today's expenses.
  • Before leaving: the top bar says All saved online.

Warehouse

  • Enter everything that arrived (Purchase).
  • Print and stick labels.
  • Send parcels; print the dispatch note.
  • Before leaving: All saved online.

Owner

  • Look at Home: is every shop open and selling?
  • Parcels with missing pieces? (Activity)
  • Once a week: download a backup.

10. Problems and questions

Someone forgot their passcode

On the passcode screen they tap Forgot your passcode?; the owner approves with the link sent to the owner's email. Or: they ask the owner to give them a new password (Settings → Users), Sign out and sign in again, and choose a new passcode.

A computer was lost, stolen or replaced

Settings → Users → Remove computer on that user's row. On the new computer, the user signs in with their user ID and password (2.4).

The top bar is red: "1 not saved"

Tap it. You will see what the cloud refused and why — usually something switched off for that place or person. Do it again on a device where it's allowed, then clear the list.

"Velanzo is already open in another tab"

Velanzo can be open only once on a computer. Close the other tab or window.

A new shop opens

Settings → Shops & warehouse → Add a place, add its people, then connect its computer (2.4).

Long barcodes look like 8.9E+12 in Excel

Format the barcode column as Text before saving a file to import.

Starting again on a computer

your name → This device: Download everything again (keeps nothing waiting behind), or Remove Velanzo from this device.
